The process

Seven steps. None of them are “build a brand first.”

A simple, repeatable process to go from idea to your first sale.

01 · Find
Find a problem you can already help with
Skip the hunt for a secret niche. Start with something you can explain to one person.
02 · Choose
Choose one simple product type
A short guide, a checklist, a template, or a prompt pack. One format. Small enough to finish.
03 · Offer
Write a one-sentence offer
Who it’s for, what they get, and what it helps them do. If you can’t say it in one line, it’s still too big.
04 · Build
Make a first version you can finish
Useful beats polished. Use free tools. Ship a version you would be willing to sell this week.
05 · Price
Set a small, honest price
The point is a real checkout, not a perfect offer. A small price makes the first attempt easier to make.
06 · Launch
Put it in front of people
A link, a post, a few conversations. You are collecting a first-sale attempt, not building a company overnight.
07 · Improve
Keep what worked,
drop what did not
Look at what people asked, ignored, or bought. Then make the next version smaller and clearer.
